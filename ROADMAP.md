# AIC Roadmap

Repository: Active Invariant Cloning Lab

Status: Active

Purpose: Define the planned progression from repository initialization to experimental validation.

---

# Guiding Principle

The purpose of this repository is not to prove Active Invariant Cloning.

The purpose is to determine whether Active Invariant Cloning can be implemented, tested, measured, and verified.

Evidence determines outcomes.

# Completion Condition

The repository reaches completion when sufficient evidence exists to evaluate the central research question:

Can a governing invariant be actively cloned while remaining live, binding, refusal-capable, and semantically equivalent across mutation-capable paths?
