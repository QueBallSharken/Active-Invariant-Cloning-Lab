Archive

Purpose

This directory preserves historical repository artifacts.

Archived materials remain part of repository history but are no longer considered active work.

---

Archive Categories

Examples may include:

- Historical research records
- Deprecated experiments
- Superseded findings
- Retired prototypes
- Historical notes

---

Preservation Rule

Historical artifacts should not be silently removed.

Repository history has value.

Research chronology has value.

Prior failures have value.

---

Repository Rule

Archive status does not imply incorrectness.

Archive status indicates that the material is no longer the active source of truth.

Active repository artifacts remain authoritative.

---

Final Statement

The archive exists to preserve continuity, provenance, and research history.
