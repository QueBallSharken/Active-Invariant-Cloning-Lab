Collaboration Protocol

Goal

Maintain repository continuity while allowing independent contribution, experimentation, testing, and verification.

The repository exists to discover whether Active Invariant Cloning (AIC) can be realized.

Collaboration should increase evidence, reduce ambiguity, and preserve continuity.

---

Contributor Responsibilities

Before making changes:

1. Read README.md
2. Read MASTER_HANDOFF.md
3. Read CHANGE_CONTROL.md
4. Read PROJECT_STRUCTURE.md
5. Read SESSION_HANDOFF.md
6. Review active objectives
7. Review known blockers

Only then begin work.

---

Change Requirements

Every significant change should answer:

What problem is being solved?

Why is the change necessary?

What files are affected?

What risks are introduced?

How is continuity preserved?

How will the result be tested?

---

Repository Principle

Evidence takes priority over opinion.

Implementation takes priority over speculation.

Verification takes priority over assumptions.

Repository continuity takes priority over conversation continuity.

---

Architectural Rule

Do not introduce new foundational concepts without justification.

Do not replace established concepts without evidence.

Do not rename core concepts without justification.

Repository direction should remain stable until existing claims have been tested.

---

Experimental Work

Experimental work belongs in:

research/

or

prototype/

until validated.

Experimental results should be documented whether they succeed or fail.

Failure is evidence.

---

Testing Requirement

Whenever practical:

- Claims should be tested.
- Results should be recorded.
- Evidence should be preserved.
- Assumptions should be challenged.

The objective is discovery.

Not confirmation.

---

Repository Priority

Evidence

↓

Implementation

↓

Testing

↓

Measurement

↓

Documentation

↓

Discussion

---

Session Requirement

SESSION_HANDOFF.md must be updated before ending significant work sessions.

Repository continuity depends on accurate handoff information.

---

Contributor Conduct

Contributors are encouraged to:

- Challenge assumptions
- Stress-test claims
- Document failures
- Document successes
- Identify hidden risks
- Preserve evidence

The goal is not to defend a theory.

The goal is to determine whether the theory is true.

---

Final Rule

Truth is the objective.

Evidence is the standard.

Repository artifacts remain authoritative.
