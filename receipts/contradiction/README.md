# Contradiction Receipts

Repository: Active Invariant Cloning Lab

Status: Active

Purpose: Preserve evidence of contradiction, disagreement, divergence, or inconsistency between active invariant clones.

---

# What Is A Contradiction Receipt?

A contradiction receipt is an evidence artifact documenting a situation where participating clones did not produce identical governance outcomes.

Contradictions are important because they may indicate:

- Semantic drift
- Translation failure
- Hidden mutation paths
- Clone corruption
- Governance failure
- Implementation defects
- Previously unknown system behavior

A contradiction is not automatically a failure.

A contradiction is evidence.

---

# Evidence Principle

Agreement is informative.

Disagreement is also informative.

The repository should preserve both.

Contradictions may reveal conditions that agreement cannot.

---

# Receipt Contents

Whenever practical, contradiction receipts should include:

- Receipt identifier
- Timestamp
- Request
- Participating clones
- Individual clone decisions
- Nature of contradiction
- Final outcome
- Supporting references

# Contradiction Categories

## Decision Contradiction

Different approval or refusal outcomes.

---

## Semantic Contradiction

Equivalent requests interpreted differently.

---

## Temporal Contradiction

Different outcomes produced at different times.

---

## Representation Contradiction

Equivalent information represented differently across clones.

---

## Unknown Contradiction

Observed disagreement without a known explanation.

Requires investigation.
