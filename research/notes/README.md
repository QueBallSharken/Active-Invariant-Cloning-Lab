# Notes

## Purpose

This directory contains research notes, observations, questions, ideas, and exploratory analysis related to Active Invariant Cloning (AIC).

Notes are working artifacts.

Notes are not automatically validated.

Notes are not automatically findings.

Notes may later contribute to experiments, specifications, prototypes, or findings.

---

# Appropriate Content

Examples include:

- Research observations
- Open questions
- Design considerations
- Experiment ideas
- Failure analysis
- Review notes
- Adversarial observations
- Literature observations

---

# Note Status

Notes may contain:

- Incomplete ideas
- Unverified assumptions
- Preliminary conclusions
- Candidate approaches

Such content should not be treated as established repository truth.

---

# Relationship To Findings

Notes generate questions.

Experiments generate evidence.

Findings summarize evidence.

Repository conclusions should be derived from evidence rather than notes.

---

# Repository Rule

Exploration is encouraged.

Speculation must be distinguishable from evidence.

Evidence remains authoritative.

---

# Continuity Rule

Important observations should be preserved even when they later prove incorrect.

Historical reasoning may provide value for future contributors.

---

# Final Statement

This directory exists to support exploration while preserving the distinction between ideas and evidence.

Evidence wins.
