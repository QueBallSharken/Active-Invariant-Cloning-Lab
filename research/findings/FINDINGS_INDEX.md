# Repository Rule

A finding is not established because it was proposed.

A finding is established because evidence supports it.

Evidence remains authoritative.

---

# Finding Classification

## Confirmed

Supported by reproducible evidence.

---

## Provisional

Supported by limited evidence.

Additional validation required.

---

## Refuted

Evidence contradicts the original claim.

Refuted findings remain valuable and should be preserved.

---

## Inconclusive

Evidence is insufficient to support a conclusion.

Further testing required.
