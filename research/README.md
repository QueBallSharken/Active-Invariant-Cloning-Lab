Research

Purpose

This directory contains exploratory work related to Active Invariant Cloning (AIC).

Research artifacts may include:

- Experiments
- Notes
- Findings
- Prior-art analysis
- Hypotheses
- Observations

Research artifacts are not automatically considered validated.

Validation requires testing and evidence.

---

Repository Rule

Research may:

- Explore
- Question
- Challenge
- Investigate

Research may not silently redefine repository claims.

Any significant changes to repository direction must follow repository governance procedures.

---

Evidence Rule

Research generates hypotheses.

Testing generates evidence.

Evidence determines conclusions.

---

Final Statement

Research exists to discover truth.

Not to defend assumptions.
