Open Questions

Purpose

This document records unresolved questions relevant to Active Invariant Cloning (AIC).

Questions remain open until:

- Evidence resolves them
- Testing resolves them
- Measurement resolves them
- The repository owner explicitly closes them

Open questions should not be silently removed.

Resolved questions should be documented.
